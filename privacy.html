Privacy Policy

At QuickKB Pro, user privacy is our top priority. All image resizing, signature cleaning, ID card merging, and PDF compression tasks run 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

We do not upload, store, view, or share any photos, signatures, Aadhar cards, or marksheets on any external server. Once you close or refresh the page, your processed data is cleared from memory.

We may use standard analytics and Google AdSense cookies to improve site performance and display relevant advertisements.
